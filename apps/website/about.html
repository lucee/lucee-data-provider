About Lucee

An open source CFML server, led by a non-profit association.

Philosophy

A primary aim of Lucee is to provide the functionality of CFML using fewer resources while providing better performance. The Lucee project follows the core development philosophy of "treat slowness as a bug".

Lucee also tries to resolve many inconsistencies found in traditional CFML, either through forced changes in behaviour or through options in the Lucee Administrator. Lucee is a community project, and the team welcomes feedback.

The organisation

The Lucee project is led by the Lucee Association Switzerland, a non-profit Swiss association based on the Swiss Civil Code. Its members are companies, and each member company has one vote. Members can be represented on the management board and help decide the direction of the project.

See the member companies. If your company would like to join, write to hello@lucee.org.
