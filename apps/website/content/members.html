Members

The Lucee Association Switzerland promotes, supports and advances the Lucee open source project. Its members are companies from around the world.

Become a member

Member companies help fund the development of Lucee and help direct its future. Each member company has a vote at the general assembly, can be elected to the board, gets access to the Lucee Slack team for internal discussions, and is listed on this site with a link to its homepage.

To join, become a member through Open Collective or write to hello@lucee.org.
